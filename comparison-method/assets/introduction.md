# Study

In this study, we investigate **how different visualization strategies support the comparison of time-varying node attributes in hierarchical network structures**.

The data used in this study is based on **an electrical grid / neighborhood**. The nodes in this neighborhood represent households. Each household has a voltage measurement that changes over time, meaning that each node in the network is associated with a time series.

An important task when analyzing electrical grid / neighborhood is to compare the grid / neighborhood under different conditions. For example, adding solar panels to households may affect voltage levels across the grid / neighborhood over time. 

In this study, you will complete a series of comparison tasks. In each task, you will compare two sets of data and answer questions based on the information shown in the visualization.

# Tasks
You will first read a short tutorial and practice several tasks to become familiar with the visualization and task format. You will then complete a series of comparison tasks using three different visual comparison strategies. 
Task responses and completion times will be recorded.

The study explores how different visualization strategies support the comparison of two datasets. Please answer each task as accurately as possible based on the information shown in the visualization.
