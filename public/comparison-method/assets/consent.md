## Visual comparison of time-varying node attributes in hierarchical network structures
You are invited to participate in a research study. 
The purpose of this study is to investigate 
how different visualization strategies support the comparison 
of time-varying data in hierarchical network structures.

## Tasks
The test contains 54 comparison tasks, which take approximately 45 minutes.

## Data and privacy
We will collect your task responses and interaction data. 
No personally identifying information, audio, video, or screen recordings will be collected. 
Your data will be anonymized, securely stored, and used only for research purposes.


## Risks and benefits
The study involves minimal risk. 
Your participation may contribute to future visualization research.
