#### **Variation questions** 
Variation questions ask which dataset shows greater variation over time: 
<img src="comparison-method/assets/exp/variation.png" alt="drawing" style="width:500px;"/>

* **Variation line:** check the line in the box, the line above the dashed line indicates the variation of B - A > 0 which means that variation of B is greater than A; while line below the dashed line indicates the variation of B - A < 0 which means variation of B is less than A.

*Answers: Dataset A, Dataset B, or Same.*
