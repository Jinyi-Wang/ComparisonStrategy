# Study

In this study, we investigate **how different visualization strategies support the comparison of time-varying data in hierarchical network structures**.

The data used in this study is based on **an electrical grid**. The nodes in this grid representing households. Each household has voltages that change over time, meaning that each node in the network is associated with a time series data

An important task when analyzing electrical grids is to compare the grid under different conditions. For example, adding solar panels to households may affect voltage levels across the grid over time. Comparing two conditions can help reveal **where, when, and how the voltage patterns differ across the network**.

In this study, you will complete a series of comparison tasks. In each task, you will compare two sets of data and answer questions based on the information shown in the visualization.

You will first complete a short tutorial and several practice tasks to become familiar with the visualization and task format. You will then complete a series of comparison tasks using several visualization designs. Please answer each task as accurately and quickly as you can.

There are no right or wrong answers. We are interested in understanding how different visualization designs support in comparing two datasets.
