# Juxtaposition

In this study, we investigate **how different visualization strategies support comparison of time-varying data**.

In this study, you will complete a series of comparison tasks. In each task, you will compare two sets of data and answer a question based on the information shown in the visualization.

You will first complete a short tutorial and several practice tasks to become familiar with the visualization and task format. You will then complete a series of comparison tasks using several visualization designs. Please answer each task as accurately and quickly as you can.

There are no right or wrong strategies. We are interested in how people use the different visualizations to complete the tasks.
